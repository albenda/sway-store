  • בטופס ההזמנה כל שדה מתויג, ושגיאות מוצגות בטקסט ליד השדה.

Accessibility statement

We want everyone to be able to browse and order on the Sway site with ease. The site follows the Israeli standard IS 5568 and WCAG 2.1 level AA.

What we did

  • The whole site works with a keyboard, including a skip-to-content link.
  • Images have text descriptions; purely decorative images are hidden from screen readers.
  • Sufficient colour contrast between text and background.
  • Built for Hebrew (right to left) and English, on every screen size.
  • Every order form field is labelled, and errors are shown as text next to the field.
  • If your system asks for reduced motion, the site shows no animations.
  • The accessibility button in the corner offers keyboard navigation, stop animations, high contrast, larger and smaller text, a readable font, and highlighted headings and links.

Known limitations

  • The 3D and AR view is visual by nature. All sizes are also given as text and in the "Will it fit?" calculator.
  • The film at the top of the page is decorative. All of its text is also available as plain text.

If you run into a problem, please tell us and we will fix it.
